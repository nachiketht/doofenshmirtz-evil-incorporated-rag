# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Executive Escape Blimp Protocol — Version 1.0
**TOP SECRET — EYES OF HEINZ DOOFENSHMIRTZ ONLY**

## 1. Purpose

This protocol explains how the CEO escapes when a scheme is foiled by Perry the Platypus, which happens every time.

## 2. Scope

This protocol applies to the CEO and to Norm, who pilots the blimp when the CEO is too busy shaking his fist.

## 3. Escape Blimp

**3.1 Location.** The escape blimp is hidden behind the giant neon 'D' on the roof, folded into a box labeled 'Holiday Decorations'.

**3.2 Launch Phrase.** The blimp inflates when the CEO shouts 'Curse you, Perry the Platypus!' within 3 feet of the box.

## 4. Destinations

The blimp flies to the backup lair in the abandoned miniature golf course, then circles until the nemesis goes home for dinner.

## 5. Acknowledgment

Knowing this protocol constitutes acknowledgment that you will never, ever store real holiday decorations in that box.
