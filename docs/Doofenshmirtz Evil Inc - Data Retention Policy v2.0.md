# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Data Retention Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 extends camera footage retention after the company repeatedly deleted the only evidence of who keeps stealing the foosball.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers paper records, digital files, inator logs and security camera footage.

## 3. Retention Periods

Records are now kept for the following periods.

| Record Type | Retention Period |
| --- | --- |
| Financial records | 7 years |
| Inator test logs | 5 years |
| Security camera footage | 180 days |
| Monologue recordings | 30 days |
| Meeting minutes | 5 years |
| Feedback on chatbot answers | 1 year |

Footage that shows a platypus is now kept for 3 years.

## 4. Destruction

Records past their retention period are shredded. Shredding by Destroy-Paper-inator is permitted only outdoors.

## 5. Legal Holds

Records under a legal hold may not be destroyed, even if they are very embarrassing.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Creating any record constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
