# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Incident Report IR-0071 Elevator Monologue Overrun — Version 1.0

## 1. Summary

The CEO began a monologue about his childhood in Gimmelshtump in the main elevator and refused to let the doors open until he had finished. Nine employees were trapped for 47 minutes.

## 2. Timeline

- 12:01 p.m. Monologue begins between floors three and four
- 12:30 p.m. Employees begin sharing snacks
- 12:48 p.m. Monologue ends with the words 'and that is why I hate lawn gnomes'
## 3. Root Cause

There was no rule limiting monologues in shared spaces.

## 4. Corrective Actions

**4.1 Elevator Rule.** The Elevator Monologue Rule was added to HR Policy 3.0.

**4.2 Snack Kit.** A snack kit is now stored in every elevator.

## 5. Sign-Off

Reviewed by the Incident Commander and filed with Incident Response. A joke was included in the cover email, per HR Policy.
