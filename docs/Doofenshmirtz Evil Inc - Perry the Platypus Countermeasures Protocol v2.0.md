# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Perry the Platypus Countermeasures Protocol — Version 2.0
**TOP SECRET — EYES OF HEINZ DOOFENSHMIRTZ ONLY**

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 upgrades every trap after Agent P escaped Operation Fedora Drop forty-three times in a row, a statistic the CEO has asked us to stop mentioning.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to the CEO and to the three employees cleared for Nemesis Operations. Everyone else should stop reading and go have some cake.

## 3. Identification

**3.1 Field Appearance.** Agent P is a teal platypus who wears a brown fedora and walks upright on two legs. Without the fedora he is a mindless pet who just sort of lies there and does nothing.

**3.2 Signature Sound.** Agent P announces himself with a low chattering sound. If you hear the chatter, an inator will be destroyed within the hour.

## 4. Trap Specifications

**4.1 Standard Trap.** The standard trap is now a self-sealing bubble of industrial-grade bubblegum that inflates around the nemesis when he lands on the balcony.

**4.2 Trap Load Rating.** Every trap must now hold at least 900 pounds of determined platypus, up from 400 pounds.

**4.3 Trap Code Name.** The trap program is now code-named Operation Bubblegum Bowler. Operation Fedora Drop is retired.

## 5. Monologue Timing

Once Agent P is trapped, the CEO may begin the monologue only after the inator is fully charged, which takes 4 minutes, so that the escape at minute eleven is less of a problem.

## 6. Decoy Program

Three decoy inators are placed on lower floors on every scheme day. The real inator is always on the top floor, behind the purple curtain, because Agent P never checks the curtain.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Reading this document constitutes acknowledgment that you will tell nobody, especially not anyone wearing a fedora. Curse you, Perry the Platypus.
