# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Time & Usage Policy — Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 3.0 responds to the foosball token economy of Version 2.0, which turned the break room into a hedge fund. It keeps the core recreation limits and replaces winner-takes-tokens with something finance can explain to auditors.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, interns and reanimated former staff. The Chief Token Officer remains, by charter, beyond policy.

## 3. Video Game Time

**3.1 Daily Allowance.** Video game time increases to 60 minutes per workday, taken in increments of no fewer than 15 minutes.

**3.2 Suspension Conditions.** Video game time is suspended for any employee who set something on fire that day, literally, figuratively, or with an inator.

## 4. Foosball Time

**4.1 Daily Allowance.** Foosball remains capped at 20 minutes per employee per day, split across no more than two sessions.

**4.2 Winner Bonus.** The winner-takes-tokens rule is abolished. The winner of a monitored match now receives a flat bonus of 10,000 tokens and the loser keeps their balance.

## 5. Token Allocation

**5.1 Allocation Amount.** Every employee is issued 750,000 tokens at the start of each six-hour cycle, up from 500,000 under Version 2.0.

**5.2 Limited Rollover.** Up to 100,000 unused tokens may now roll over into the next cycle. Finance describes this as a compromise and the CTO describes it as a betrayal.

## 6. Requesting Additional Tokens

Requests for supplemental tokens still go to the Chief Token Officer, whose discretion remains total. There is still no appeals process.

## 7. Token Depletion — Consequences

An employee who runs out of tokens and is denied supplemental tokens must perform skincare for the rest of the cycle. Skincare stations are now also stocked with cucumber slices.

## 8. Acknowledgment

Continued presence at the foosball table constitutes acknowledgment of this policy and a promise to stop calling it an asset class.
