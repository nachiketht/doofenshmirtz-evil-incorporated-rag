# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Building and Lair Maintenance Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 speeds up urgent repairs and finally adds signs for trap doors, after the third visitor fell into the alligator pit.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers all company buildings, lairs, secret passages and trap doors.

## 3. Maintenance Requests

**3.1 Request Process.** Maintenance requests are submitted through the Facilities portal or by pressing the big purple help button.

**3.2 Response Times.** Facilities now responds to urgent requests within 1 hour, down from 4 hours, and to routine requests within 3 business days.

## 4. Trap Doors

**4.1 Inspection.** Every trap door is inspected weekly. Trap doors over the alligator pit are inspected daily.

**4.2 Signage.** Trap doors in public hallways must now be marked with a small sign reading 'Probably Fine'.

## 5. Lair Cleaning

The volcano lair is cleaned weekly. Lava is not considered a cleaning product.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Occupying any company building constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
