# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Laboratory Safety Policy — Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 3.0 adds rules for portal experiments, which are now frequent enough to need their own section.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to anyone entering a laboratory, including visitors, interns, and robots on cleaning duty.

## 3. Protective Equipment

**3.1 Required Gear.** Safety goggles and a lab coat are required in every laboratory. Swim goggles are not safety goggles, no matter how tightly they fit.

**3.2 Ray Testing Gear.** Anyone testing a ray must also wear a mirrored visor, in case the ray is reflected back by a shiny surface or a smug platypus.

## 4. Inator Testing

**4.1 Test Targets.** Inators may be tested only on approved targets, such as watermelons, mannequins, and the decommissioned photocopier.

**4.2 Countdown Rule.** Every test must be preceded by a spoken countdown from five. Shouting 'behold' does not replace the countdown.

## 5. Spills and Accidents

Spills must now be reported within 5 minutes, down from 15 minutes. Transformations into animals, plants or furniture must be reported immediately by the nearest buddy.

## 6. Buddy System

Nobody may work alone in a laboratory. Each employee must have a lab buddy who checks on them every 20 minutes and confirms they are still a person.

## 7. Portal Experiments

**7.1 Portal Limits.** Only one portal may be open in a laboratory at a time. Portals to other dimensions must be closed before lunch.

**7.2 Alternate Selves.** Employees who meet an alternate version of themselves must not ask it to cover their shift.

## 8. Acknowledgment

Entering a laboratory constitutes acknowledgment of this policy and a promise never to lick anything glowing.
