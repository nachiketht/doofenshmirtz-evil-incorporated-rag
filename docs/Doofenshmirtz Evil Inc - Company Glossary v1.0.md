# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Company Glossary — Version 1.0

## 1. About This Glossary

Terms used across Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated documents.

## 2. Terms

**2.1 Inator.** Any device built to do something evil. The name must end in '-inator'.

**2.2 Tri-State Area.** The region the company is trying to take over, one inator at a time.

**2.3 Gimmelshtump.** The CEO's home village, source of most backstories and an extra per diem.

**2.4 Focus Token.** The internal currency spent on tasks, deliverables and deep thoughts.

**2.5 The Nemesis.** The company's one and only nemesis. Further details are classified.
