# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Animal Visitor Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs animals visiting headquarters. It exists for a reason that everyone knows and nobody will say out loud.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to pets, service animals, minion mascots, and any animal that arrives by crashing through a skylight.

## 3. Pet Visits

**3.1 Bring Your Pet Day.** Employees may bring a pet to work on the first Friday of each month. Pets must be leashed, crated, or otherwise contained.

**3.2 Prohibited Animals.** Prohibited animals include sharks, alligators, and any animal wearing a hat.

## 4. Platypus Sightings

**4.1 Reporting.** Any platypus seen inside the building must be reported to Facilities at once, with a description of its hat, if any.

**4.2 Response.** Employees should not approach the platypus. They should calmly move away from any inator and alert the CEO, who will want to know.

## 5. Allergies

Employees with allergies may request a pet-free floor. The seventh floor is permanently pet-free, except for the ooze, which is not technically a pet.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Bringing an animal to work constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
