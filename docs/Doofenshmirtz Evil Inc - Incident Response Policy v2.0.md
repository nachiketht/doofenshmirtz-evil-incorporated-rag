# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Incident Response Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 speeds up nemesis response and shortens the reporting window, after a Sev 2 intrusion turned into a Sev 1 while everyone was looking for the alarm button.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers security breaches, inator malfunctions, nemesis intrusions, and any explosion larger than a popcorn bag.

## 3. Severity Levels

Every incident is assigned a severity level.

| Level | Example | Response Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sev 1 | Inator aimed at the Tri-State Area by mistake | Immediate |
| Sev 2 | Nemesis intrusion in progress | 5 minutes |
| Sev 3 | Minor explosion in a lab | 1 hour |
| Sev 4 | Someone took the last donut | Next business day |

Alarm buttons are now installed in every room, including the elevators.

## 4. Response Steps

**4.1 Declare.** Any employee may declare an incident by pressing the red alarm button and shouting the severity level.

**4.2 Command.** The Incident Commander is the most senior person present who is not currently trapped.

## 5. Post-Incident Report

An incident report must now be written within 2 business days for every Sev 1 or Sev 2 incident, down from 3 days.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Surviving any incident constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
