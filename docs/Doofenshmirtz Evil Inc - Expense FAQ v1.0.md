Quick answers about expenses under Expense Reimbursement Policy 3.0.

## 2. Claims

**2.1 Deadline.** Q: How long do I have to submit a claim? A: 14 days from the purchase, with an itemized receipt.

**2.2 Fedoras.** Q: Can I expense a fedora? A: No. Fedoras are never reimbursable, for reasons the CEO describes as personal.

## 3. Expense Card
