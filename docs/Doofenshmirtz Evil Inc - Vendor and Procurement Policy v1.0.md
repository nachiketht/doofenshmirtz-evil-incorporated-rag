# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Vendor and Procurement Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how the company buys things from other companies, including the many questionable suppliers of the Tri-State Area evil-supply market.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every purchase made with company money, from staplers to shrink rays.

## 3. Approved Vendors

**3.1 Vendor List.** Purchases should be made from the approved vendor list, which includes Evil Supplies Depot, Lasers R Us, and Gary's Discount Self-Destruct Buttons.

**3.2 New Vendors.** A new vendor must pass a background check and confirm in writing that it is not a front for O.W.C.A.

## 4. Quotes

**4.1 Three-Quote Rule.** Purchases above 5,000 dollars require three written quotes. A quote from a vendor who laughs maniacally during the call counts as half a quote.

**4.2 Sole Source.** Single-vendor purchases are allowed only when the part is unique, such as a replacement crystal for a de-love-inator.

## 5. Payment Terms

Vendors are paid within 45 days of invoice. Vendors demanding payment in the form of a captured platypus are removed from the list.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Anyone who buys anything acknowledges this policy, especially the person who bought forty rubber chickens last spring.
