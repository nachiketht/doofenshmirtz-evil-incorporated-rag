# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Travel Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 shortens the booking window and adds rules for travel through portals, which are cheaper but harder to expense.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all travel paid for by the company.

## 3. Booking

**3.1 Advance Booking.** Flights must now be booked at least 21 days in advance, up from 14 days. Blimp travel still needs 3 days of notice.

**3.2 Class of Travel.** Employees fly economy, and the CEO may still fly by rocket at his own risk.

## 4. Lodging

Hotels are booked through the travel desk. Hotels shaped like a volcano are preferred but not required.

## 5. Villain Summits

Attendance at villain summits requires manager approval and a promise not to sign up for any joint scheme without Legal.

## 6. Portal Travel

Travel by portal is allowed only to destinations in this dimension. Receipts from other dimensions are not accepted.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Booking any trip constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
