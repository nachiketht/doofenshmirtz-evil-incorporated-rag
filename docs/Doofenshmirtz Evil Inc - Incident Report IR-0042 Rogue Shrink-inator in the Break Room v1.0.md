# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Incident Report IR-0042 Rogue Shrink-inator in the Break Room — Version 1.0

## 1. Summary

At 3:04 p.m. the Shrink-inator was accidentally fired in the break room, shrinking the coffee machine, two chairs and the deputy head of Finance to roughly the size of a teacup. The inator was meant for a scheme against the tallest building in the Tri-State Area. Severity was set to Sev 3.

## 2. Timeline

- 3:04 p.m. Shrink-inator fired while being carried past the microwave
- 3:06 p.m. Deputy head of Finance reported the incident by shouting very quietly
- 3:40 p.m. Reverse setting located in the manual, on page 112
- 4:15 p.m. Everyone and everything restored except one chair
## 3. Root Cause

The Shrink-inator was carried with its safety cap off. The trigger was pressed by an elbow while the carrier reached for a donut.

## 4. Corrective Actions

**4.1 Safety Caps.** Inators must be carried with the safety cap on, always.

**4.2 Break Room Ban.** Inators are banned from the break room. The small chair is kept on the CEO's desk as a reminder.

## 5. Sign-Off

Reviewed by the Incident Commander and filed with Incident Response. A joke was included in the cover email, per HR Policy.
