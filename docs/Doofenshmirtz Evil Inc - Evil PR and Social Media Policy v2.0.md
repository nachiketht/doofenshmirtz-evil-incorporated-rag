# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Evil PR and Social Media Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 speeds up media responses after a news crew filmed the Giant Floating Baby Head for six hours with no comment from the company.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to anyone speaking for the company, posting about the company, or appearing on the news near a smoking crater.

## 3. Social Media

**3.1 Approved Accounts.** Only the Evil PR team may post on official company accounts. Posts must include at least one evil laugh, written as 'mwahaha'.

**3.2 Personal Accounts.** Employees may mention they work here, but may not post photos of unfinished inators or the CEO's baby pictures.

## 4. Media Requests

All media requests must now reach Evil PR within 2 hours, down from 24 hours. Interviews during schemes remain prohibited.

## 5. Slogans

The official slogan is 'Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated: Evil, But Incorporated'. New slogans must be approved by the CEO and tested on Norm.

## 6. Influencers

The company may sponsor villain influencers with more than 10,000 followers, provided they never film the self-destruct button.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Speaking about the company in public constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
