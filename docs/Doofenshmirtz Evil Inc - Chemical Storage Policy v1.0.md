# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Chemical Storage Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains where and how chemicals are stored, so that nobody confuses the Evil Goo with the coffee creamer again.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers every chemical, goo, ooze, and unexplained glowing liquid on company premises.

## 3. Storage Rules

**3.1 Labeling.** Every container must be labeled with its contents and the date it was made. Labels that only say 'DO NOT' are not sufficient.

**3.2 Separation.** Flammable chemicals are stored in the red cabinet and anything that hums is stored in the blue cabinet.

## 4. Quantity Limits

No laboratory may store more than 20 liters of flammable liquid outside the red cabinet.

## 5. Disposal

Chemicals are disposed of through Lab Safety. Pouring goo down the drain is prohibited because the drain has filed a complaint.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Handling any chemical constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
