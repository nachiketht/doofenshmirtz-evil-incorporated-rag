# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Minion Scheduling Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets shift schedules for minions, henchpeople and robot staff, so that someone is always available to guard the inator and someone else is always available to fetch snacks.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all minions, henchpeople, Norm-bots and temporary goons hired for large schemes.

## 3. Shifts

**3.1 Shift Length.** Minion shifts are 8 hours long, with a 30-minute lunch and two 10-minute breaks for menacing glances.

**3.2 Night Shift.** At least two minions must guard the lair overnight. Night-shift minions receive a 15 percent pay bonus and a nightlight.

## 4. Shift Swaps

**4.1 Swap Requests.** Minions may swap shifts with each other if both agree and the swap is posted on the scheduling board at least 48 hours in advance.

**4.2 Robot Swaps.** Robots may not swap shifts with humans, because Norm keeps volunteering for every shift.

## 5. Scheme Days

On days when a major scheme is active, all minions are scheduled for a full shift and paid overtime for any hour spent being chased.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Clocking in for any shift constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
