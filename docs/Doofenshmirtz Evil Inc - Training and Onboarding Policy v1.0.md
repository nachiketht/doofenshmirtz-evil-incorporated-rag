# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Training and Onboarding Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how new henchpeople, scientists and interns are trained, so that nobody presses the wrong button on their first day.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every new hire and every employee changing roles.

## 3. Onboarding

**3.1 First Week.** New hires spend their first week shadowing a senior employee and learning where all the trap doors are.

**3.2 Required Courses.** Required courses are Lab Safety Basics, Evil Laughing for Beginners, and Recognizing a Platypus in Disguise.

## 4. Ongoing Training

Every employee completes at least 10 hours of training per year. Watching the CEO's old home videos counts for 1 hour, maximum.

## 5. Certificates

Completed courses earn a certificate signed by the CEO with a purple pen.

## 6. Acknowledgment

Attending any training constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
