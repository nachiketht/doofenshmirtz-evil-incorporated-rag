# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Henchperson Uniform Policy — Version 2.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 2.0 switched the uniform to bright orange for visibility. This version was retired after henchpeople were mistaken for traffic cones across the Tri-State Area, and the whole policy is kept only for the audit trail.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all henchpeople and minions working on scheme days.

## 3. Uniform Items

**3.1 Standard Uniform.** The standard uniform became a bright orange jumpsuit with reflective stripes and an orange helmet.

**3.2 Footwear.** Henchpeople wear orange boots with soft soles.

## 4. Laundry

Uniforms are laundered weekly by Facilities. Uniforms that come back a different color must be worn anyway until replacements arrive.

## 5. Acknowledgment

Putting on the uniform constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
