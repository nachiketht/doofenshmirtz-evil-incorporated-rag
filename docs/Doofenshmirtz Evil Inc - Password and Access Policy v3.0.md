# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Password and Access Policy — Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 3.0 drops forced password rotation in line with modern guidance and introduces retinal scanners, which were already installed for dramatic effect and are now finally plugged in.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every account on every company system, including the inator control panels and the Norm-bot maintenance terminal.

## 3. Password Rules

**3.1 Length.** Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Four-word passphrases remain encouraged.

**3.2 Rotation.** Scheduled 90-day rotation is abolished. Passwords must be changed only when a compromise is suspected, or when a platypus was seen near your desk.

**3.3 Banned Passwords.** The banned list is now checked automatically against a list of known breached passwords and every inator name.

## 4. Badge Access

**4.1 Badge Display.** Employee badges must be visible at all times inside the building. Badges worn on a fedora do not count as visible.

**4.2 Tailgating.** Employees may not hold secure doors open for anyone, including adorable teal animals who appear to be lost.

## 5. Lost Devices

Lost laptops and phones must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours. Devices lost inside an inator's blast radius must be reported immediately.

## 6. Multi-Factor Authentication

**6.1 Requirement.** Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the inator control panels, and the payroll system.

**6.2 Approved Second Factors.** Approved second factors are an authenticator app or a hardware key. Answering a riddle posed by Norm is not an approved second factor.

## 7. Retinal Scanners

Retinal scanners now guard the server room and the inator vault. Employees wearing an eye patch for dramatic effect must remove it before scanning.

## 8. Acknowledgment

Logging in to any company system constitutes acknowledgment of this policy and a promise not to write your password on a sticky note shaped like a platypus.
