# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Parking and Blimp Docking Policy — Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 3.0 adds a third docking slot and a jetpack landing pad, after the roof became the busiest airport in the Tri-State Area.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every car, scooter, hovercraft and blimp arriving at headquarters.

## 3. Car Parking

**3.1 Assigned Spaces.** Parking spaces are assigned by seniority. The CEO's space is the one closest to the door and is painted with a large letter D.

**3.2 Visitor Parking.** Visitors park in the three spaces nearest the giant neon sign. Visitors arriving in a flying car must use the roof.

## 4. Blimp Docking

**4.1 Docking Slots.** The roof now has three blimp docking slots. Slot three is reserved for visiting villains.

**4.2 Docking Time.** Guest blimps may dock for up to 1 hour. Overstaying blimps are now towed by the company blimp, slowly and dramatically.

## 5. Towing

Vehicles parked in the CEO's space are towed immediately. Hovercraft are pushed gently instead, because they do not tow well. An inator parked in a car space is towed to the lab.

## 6. Electric Vehicle Charging

Four charging stations are available in the garage. Charging an inator at a car charger is prohibited.

## 7. Jetpack Landing

Jetpacks land only on the marked pad on the north side of the roof. Landing in the smoking area is considered rude.

## 8. Acknowledgment

Parking at headquarters constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
