# Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated
## Inator Development Lifecycle Policy — Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

Version 3.0 lengthens the prototype review and introduces an Inator Review Board, so the CEO is no longer the only approver.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every inator project, whether funded, unfunded, or built in the CEO's apartment over a weekend.

## 3. Stages

Every inator moves through these stages in order.

- Idea: a backstory explaining why the inator is needed
- Sketch: a drawing on a napkin, whiteboard or forearm
- Prototype: a version that works at least once
- Deployment: aimed at the Tri-State Area
- Destruction: usually by a platypus
Skipping the Sketch stage is prohibited after the Untitled Blob-inator incident.

## 4. Stage Gates

**4.1 Prototype Review.** Prototype reviews may now last up to 60 minutes and must include a demo on at least two watermelons.

**4.2 Deployment Approval.** Deployment requires approval from the Inator Review Board, made up of the CEO, the head of Lab Safety, and Norm, who has a tie-breaking vote.

## 5. Post-Destruction Review

After every destroyed inator, the team holds a 15-minute review covering what went wrong, who pressed the button, and whether a platypus was involved.

## 6. Platypus Resistance Review

Before deployment, every inator must survive a 10-minute test against a trained intern in a teal costume and a fedora.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Starting any inator project constitutes acknowledgment of this policy.
